# Sound Sense

1. The Problem: The Invisible Epidemic

The Gap: Standard smartphone volume warnings are one-size-fits-all (typically flagging everything above 85 dB). However, someone with early-stage acoustic damage or specific frequency sensitivities requires hyper-personalized limits.

The Cost: Traditional audiograms require expensive clinical equipment and soundproof booths, making routine screenings inaccessible to billions in low-resource communities.

2. The Solution: Dynamic, Accessible Audiology

Adaptive Testing: Instead of a rigid, slow beep-test, our AI uses a Bayesian staircase method. It listens to user feedback and intelligently micro-adjusts the next frequency and volume, cutting testing time in half while maintaining clinical accuracy.

Continuous Guardrails: The app runs quietly in the background, continuously normalizing headphone audio and scanning environmental noise via the phone's microphone to recommend safe exposure durations.

3. Social Impact & Scaling (How It Wins MIT Solve)

The Solve Alignment: Fits perfectly under Health Equity or Human Centered Technology challenges.

The B2B2C Model: The app can be distributed for free to users in low-income regions via partnerships with global public health organizations, while monetizing through premium enterprise API integrations for headphone manufacturers and industrial factory workers.
